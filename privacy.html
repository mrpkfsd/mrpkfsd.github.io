Privacy policy

gmail-news-reader is used only by its owner. With the owner's permission it reads Google Alerts emails in the owner's own Gmail account (read-only) to find the linked news articles.

All data stays on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data from any other Google user is accessed.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
